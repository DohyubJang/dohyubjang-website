ABOUT

About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher working at the interface of nanomedicine, biomaterials, and drug delivery.

My research focuses on the development of functional nanomaterials for therapeutic and diagnostic biomedical applications, including polyphenol-derived nanomaterials, plasmonic nanostructures, and bioengineered therapeutic systems.
